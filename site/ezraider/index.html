Meet the EZRaider

Why this feels different

EZRaider is a distinctive four-wheel personal electric vehicle developed in Israel. The manufacturer combines a low centre of gravity, responsive steering and a dynamic suspension to create a controlled, engaging ride. On a VOY PRO tour, your experience is guided, and you receive a practical introduction before departure.

01 · Four wheels

A planted four-wheel platform and generous footprint feel different from balancing a two-wheeled scooter. It can still tip or lose traction, so correct riding technique matters.

02 · Dynamic suspension

The manufacturer's patented steering and suspension design helps the wheels respond to uneven surfaces. Our tours follow approved routes, not extreme-terrain demonstrations.

03 · Hydraulic braking

Hydraulic disc brakes and smooth power delivery help riders manage speed. Every participant learns controlled starts and stops during the briefing.

04 · Quiet electric drive

The electric drivetrain makes the vehicle responsive without an engine's exhaust or roar. You can enjoy the city, the stops and the guide's stories.

From first look to first ride

1 · Your guide welcomes you

We introduce the vehicle, the riding position and the essential controls. The guide checks that each rider understands the instructions.

2 · A short supervised practice

You try a gentle start, steering and a controlled stop in the practice area. There is time for questions and extra guidance.

3 · Ride together

Once the guide is satisfied that the group is ready, you follow the guide at an appropriate pace with planned sightseeing stops.

Ride responsibly

Safety is a process — not a promise

The vehicle's four-wheel design supports stability, but no moving vehicle is risk-free. Surface conditions, turning, speed and rider decisions remain important. Our guided experience focuses on preparation and sensible riding.

Helmet & briefing

A helmet and a practical briefing are part of our guided setup. Follow the guide's instructions throughout.

Controlled speed

Start gently, keep both hands on the handlebars, maintain space, slow before turns and stop fully before stepping off.

Route & readiness

We check suitability for the selected route and the rider's ability to control the vehicle. Advanced tours may require prior experience.

Check before departure

Our team checks the vehicle, its controls and the operating conditions. If the route or weather makes riding unsuitable, we adapt operations.

Good to know

For Budapest guided tours, independent driving follows the current destination rider policy. Younger riders are considered only through the designated assessment and staff-approval route where permitted; otherwise children may join only as passengers, subject to the active policy and suitable child-seat fit. No road-use or licence rule from another country should be assumed to apply in Budapest.

A few useful answers

Do I need prior EZRaider experience?

No prior experience is generally required for standard guided tours. We begin with instruction and a supervised practice. Advanced routes may have additional prerequisites.

Is a four-wheel EZRaider completely safe?

No vehicle is risk-free. Its design supports stability, while safe operation still depends on speed, conditions, instructions and the rider's control.

Can I ride with my child?

An eligible adult can request a dedicated child passenger seat. Age, fit, the selected tour, current policy and available seats must all be confirmed by the team.

Can I ride the EZRaider anywhere?

No. Riding locations and permissions depend on local regulations and the approved guided route. Manufacturer demonstration videos may show locations not used by VOY PRO.

FAMILY RIDING

Bringing the little ones? We've thought about that too.

An adult may be able to ride with a child passenger using a fitted seat approved by our Budapest team. The child never controls the EZRaider. Seat assignment depends on age, physical fit, operating rules and availability.
