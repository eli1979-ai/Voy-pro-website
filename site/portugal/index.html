DETAILED ROUTES

What each Marvão route includes

These are the published route highlights from the local operator. The exact order can vary with access, weather and the pace of the group.

1 hour

Vila de Marvão Express Tour

The short route for seeing the essentials of historic Marvão.

  1. 1

    Historic Marvão

    A guided ride through the village and its historic setting.

  2. 2

    Marvão Castle

    A visit to the castle, one of the defining landmarks of the hilltop village.

  3. 3

    Short village visit

    Time to discover the character of Marvão without committing to the longer route.

  4. 4

    Historical & cultural points

    Stops and interpretation around the village’s historical and cultural points of interest.

1h30

Marvão Explorer Tour

A longer route that expands the village experience beyond the short circuit.

  1. 1

    Marvão village

    A guided exploration of the village with more time to understand its setting and character.

  2. 2

    Marvão Castle

    Visit the castle as a central part of the route.

  3. 3

    Inside the walls

    Explore the streets and points of interest within Marvão’s historic walls.

  4. 4

    Outside the walls

    Continue beyond the walled core to see more of Marvão and its immediate surroundings.

  5. 5

    Included entries

    The published Explorer route includes entry tickets connected with the visit.

2h30

Megalithic Route

The longest route combines archaeology, nature, off-road riding and local food.

  1. 1

    Historical & megalithic heritage

    Discover historical places and megalithic monuments linked to the prehistoric communities of the region.

  2. 2

    Off-road granite landscapes

    Ride off-road sections through the granite landscapes around Marvão.

  3. 3

    River Sever & the Spanish border

    Reach the River Sever, which marks the Portugal–Spain border and is one of the route’s published highlights. In suitable weather, the river stop may also allow time for a refreshing dip.

  4. 4

    Old Beirã Railway Station

    Visit the former Beirã railway station as part of the longer regional route.

  5. 5

    Local gastronomic tasting

    Finish with a tasting of local and regional products from Marvão.

THE EXPERIENCE

Why ride Marvão by EZRaider?

Village, castle and landscape

Marvão sits high in the Serra de São Mamede, combining historic streets, defensive walls, a castle and wide Alto Alentejo views.

More than a city ride

Routes range from a compact village discovery to longer outdoor experiences with nature, off-road sections and megalithic heritage.

Local partner

The experience is guided and operated locally in Marvão by Pombais Experience & Villas. VOY PRO manages the customer request and coordinates confirmation with the local operator.
