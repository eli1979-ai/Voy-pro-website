How VOY PRO processes personal data on voy-pro.com.

Last updated: 21 September 2026
Terms of Use & Booking ConditionsPrivacy NoticeCookie & Storage PolicyLegal Notice / ImprintAccessibility Statement

1. Controller

AllGo Guided Tours Bud Kft., 1052 Budapest, Városház utca 14., Hungary, is the controller for personal data processed through voy-pro.com for website operation and Budapest bookings. Contact: voy.pro.eu@gmail.com. A partner destination may act as an independent controller for information it receives to fulfil a local experience.

2. Data we process

We may process booking and request details (name, e-mail, phone, selected experience, date, group composition, guide language, payment status and booking reference), communications, waiver or audit information where required, technical security data, and analytics or attribution data only where optional analytics consent has been given.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Booking and service data are processed to take steps at your request and perform the contract. Accounting and legally required records are processed to comply with legal obligations. Security and dispute records may be processed for legitimate interests in protecting the service and legal claims. Optional analytics is processed on consent.

4. Recipients and processors

Data may be shared, where necessary, with infrastructure providers such as Vercel and Supabase, communication providers, payment providers when enabled, professional advisers and the responsible local operator where information is needed to fulfil a requested experience.

5. International transfers

Where a service provider processes data outside the EEA, an applicable transfer mechanism such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards is used where required.

6. Retention

Booking, transaction and accounting records are retained for the periods required by applicable law. Operational and support data are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for booking, service, safety, dispute and legal purposes. Browser-side optional analytics keys are removed when analytics consent is withdrawn.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing, and may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Requests can be sent to voy.pro.eu@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11., Hungary.

8. Children and minors

A minor’s participation may require a parent or legal guardian and the relevant rider documentation. We do not intentionally use children’s data for behavioural advertising.

9. Automated processes

The site may automate availability, price display, security checks and operational routing. We do not use solely automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects without an applicable legal basis and safeguards.
