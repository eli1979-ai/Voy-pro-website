Our approach

We aim to make voy-pro.com usable with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom and common assistive technologies, using WCAG 2.2 AA as a practical design target where reasonably achievable.

Measures currently in place

The site includes semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, form labels, status announcements for booking states, language and RTL metadata, responsive layouts and automated checks for missing alternative text, broken links and common accessibility regressions.

Known limitations

Some third-party services and future payment components are outside our direct technical control. The current site has not yet undergone an independent full manual accessibility audit.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact voy.pro.eu@gmail.com or +36 30 099 3099. Please identify the page and problem so we can investigate and provide an accessible alternative where possible.

Review

This statement is reviewed as the website, booking flow and applicable accessibility requirements evolve.
