Cookie & Storage Policy

Essential browser storage and optional analytics used by VOY PRO.

Last updated: 21 September 2026
Terms of Use & Booking ConditionsPrivacy NoticeCookie & Storage PolicyLegal Notice / ImprintAccessibility Statement

1. Technologies used

voy-pro.com currently relies mainly on browser local storage and similar technologies. In this notice, “cookies” is used as a convenient label for cookies and similar browser storage.

2. Strictly necessary storage

The consent record voy_consent_v1 and booking-critical keys such as voy_session_id, voy_experience_id and voy_recovery_state support booking continuity, security and recovery and may operate without analytics consent where necessary to provide the requested service.

3. Optional analytics

With consent, analytics and attribution keys may be stored, including voy_visitor_id, voy_first_attribution, voy_last_attribution, voy_segment, voy_experiments and voy_event_queue_v1. Optional analytics is disabled until the user actively allows it.

4. Changing your choice

You can change your analytics choice at any time using the control. Choosing essential-only mode removes the site’s browser-side optional analytics keys.

5. Third-party sites

Following links to WhatsApp, Tripadvisor, Google, GetYourGuide or another external service takes you to that provider’s environment, where its own privacy and cookie rules apply.
