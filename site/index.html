THE EZRAIDER EXPERIENCE

Meet the EZRaider. A different way to explore — together.

Four wheels. Quiet electric power. No windows between you and the world.

The EZRaider turns the journey into an adventure — whether you're exploring as a couple, with friends or with family.

01

Stand or sit

Enjoy an open-air ride standing or seated, depending on the vehicle configuration used on your tour.

02

Bring the little explorers

On eligible tours, children can join as passengers in approved, properly fitted child seats.

03

Different generations. One adventure.

Share the experience with a partner, friends or family members of different ages.

04

Guided from the start

Your guide introduces the controls and leads supervised practice before departure.

It's not just how you get there. It's how you feel along the way.
