CORPORATE EVENT FAQ

Questions company and team-building organizers usually ask.

How do we request a corporate or team-building plan?

WhatsApp is the recommended starting point. Send the preferred date, time window and approximate headcount, and the Budapest team will coordinate the current route, vehicles, guides and departure structure.

Can you handle a larger company group?

Yes, subject to live vehicle and staff availability. If one departure is not practical, the team can coordinate multiple departures as part of one event plan.

Can we discuss company invoicing requirements?

Yes. Include your invoicing or company-booking requirements in the enquiry and the local team will confirm the relevant booking and payment details.

What are the age rules?

Current Budapest age rules are loaded from our operating policy.

Does everyone need previous riding experience?

No. Every tour begins with a briefing and practice before departure.

What happens if weather affects the event?

Tours can operate in light rain. If conditions affect the planned departure, the local team will coordinate the relevant timing or change options under the active booking policy.
