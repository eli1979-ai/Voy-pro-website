THE EXPERIENCE

Choose an area that matches the way you want to experience Budapest

District V · central and convenient

Belváros–Lipótváros is a practical base for first-time visitors who want the Danube, Parliament and central Pest within easy reach.

Districts VI–VII · lively and well connected

Terézváros and Erzsébetváros suit travellers who want restaurants, nightlife, cafés and strong public-transport connections close by.

Buda · quieter evenings and historic surroundings

Staying on the Buda side can suit travellers who prefer a calmer base and easy access to Castle Hill, while central Pest remains a short ride away.

A SIMPLE RULE

Stay central for convenience; choose your neighbourhood for atmosphere

The exact hotel matters less than having a location that matches your plans. Check walking distance to the places you expect to revisit, late-night transport if relevant, and whether you prefer lively Pest or quieter Buda. Once you arrive, an EZRaider tour can give you a fast overview of the wider city.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions before booking.

Do I need previous experience?

No. Every tour starts with a briefing and practice, and the group departs only when everyone is comfortable.

What are the age rules?

Independent riding is generally from age 16. Child-seat and baby-seat options depend on current seat availability and the booking conditions shown for your tour.

What happens if the weather changes?

Tours can operate in light rain. If conditions affect departure, the local team will present the relevant timing or change options. The policy shown for the booking governs.

What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?

The cancellation and rescheduling terms shown during booking apply to your reservation.

Can I book a private tour or a large group?

Yes, subject to availability. For larger groups, use the group page and our local team will coordinate the right number of vehicles and guides.

Can I speak to someone before booking?

Yes. WhatsApp connects you directly with the local VOY PRO team.
