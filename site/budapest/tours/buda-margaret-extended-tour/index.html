TYPICAL ROUTE

Extended Buda + Margaret route

This longer experience combines the main Buda Castle route with the Margaret Island route. The exact sequence may vary with traffic, access, weather and group pace.

  1. 1

    Elizabeth Bridge & Buda approach

    Cross toward Buda and ride through the riverfront approach toward Castle Hill.

  2. 2

    Gellért Hill area

    Pass the Gellért Hill area before continuing deeper into historic Buda.

  3. 3

    Buda Castle & Royal Palace

    Explore the palace area and the streets of Budapest’s Castle District.

  4. 4

    Matthias Church & Fisherman’s Bastion

    Stop at the Castle District’s best-known landmarks and panoramic Danube viewpoints.

  5. 5

    Danube riverside & Parliament

    Ride back along the river and include a stop to see the Hungarian Parliament.

  6. 6

    Margaret Island promenades

    Enter the island for the greener, calmer second half of the experience.

  7. 7

    Musical Fountain & medieval ruins

    Visit two of the island’s signature sights while riding between parkland and historic remains.

  8. 8

    Small zoo, gardens & nature

    Finish with the island’s family-friendly animal area, gardens and relaxed green spaces.
