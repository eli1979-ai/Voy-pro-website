TYPICAL ROUTE

Heroes’ Square & Budapest City EZRaider Tour route & points of interest

The exact order may change with traffic, access, weather and group pace.

  1. 1

    Central Budapest

    Start from the VOY PRO base and head into the historic centre of Pest.

  2. 2

    Hungarian State Opera

    Stop at the Opera House on Andrássy Avenue, one of Budapest’s defining architectural landmarks.

  3. 3

    Andrássy Avenue

    Continue along Budapest’s grand UNESCO-listed boulevard toward the city’s monumental northeast axis.

  4. 4

    Heroes’ Square

    Reach Heroes’ Square for the Millennium Monument and the broad ceremonial cityscape.

  5. 5

    City Park area

    Explore the edge of Városliget and its landmark surroundings before turning back toward central Pest.

  6. 6

    Central Budapest return

    Return through Pest with a different perspective on the city centre.
