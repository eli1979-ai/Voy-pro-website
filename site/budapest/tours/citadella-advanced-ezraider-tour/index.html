Citadella Advanced EZRaider Tour

A more demanding Budapest route built around the climb toward Gellért Hill and the Citadella area, with elevated city views and a riding profile intended for experienced EZRaider guests.

Advanced-rider route

This route is offered only after the local team confirms rider readiness. The current operating requirement is successful completion of the Buda tour plus another qualifying EZRaider tour without riding problems. Final eligibility is confirmed by the operating team and may change with safety or route conditions.

TYPICAL ROUTE

Citadella Advanced EZRaider Tour route & points of interest

The exact order may change with traffic, access, weather and group pace.

  1. 1

    Central Budapest

    Meet the team and complete the pre-departure readiness check before leaving the city centre.

  2. 2

    Danube crossing

    Cross toward Buda and approach the Gellért Hill area.

  3. 3

    Gellért Hill ascent

    Take on the more demanding climb and road profile that make this route suitable only for approved experienced riders.

  4. 4

    Citadella area

    Reach the Citadella area for elevated views over Budapest, subject to current access restrictions and operating conditions.

  5. 5

    Panoramic viewpoints

    Pause at available viewpoints across the Danube, Buda and Pest.

  6. 6

    Controlled descent & return

    Descend with the guide and return to central Budapest at a pace matched to the group.
