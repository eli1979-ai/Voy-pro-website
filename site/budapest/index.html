THE EXPERIENCE

See more of Budapest without turning the day into a walking marathon

See more. Walk less.

A guided EZRaider experience combines the reach of an electric city tour with the pace of a small-group sightseeing experience. You ride between landmarks, stop for views and stories, and avoid spending your day walking from one attraction to the next.

The Budapest experience

The most popular route focuses on Buda Castle, Fisherman’s Bastion, Matthias Church, panoramic Danube viewpoints and the historic streets of Castle Hill. Margaret Island offers a greener, more relaxed alternative.

Easy to learn

Every tour begins with a briefing and practice session. No previous experience is required. The guide controls the pace and only departs once the group is comfortable.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions before booking.

Do I need previous experience?

No. Every tour starts with a briefing and practice, and the group departs only when everyone is comfortable.

What are the age rules?

Current Budapest age rules are loaded from our operating policy.

What happens if the weather changes?

Tours can operate in light rain. If conditions affect departure, the local team will present the relevant timing or change options. The policy shown for the booking governs.

What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?

The cancellation and rescheduling terms shown during booking apply to your reservation.

Can I book a private tour or a large group?

Yes, subject to availability. For larger groups, use the group page and our local team will coordinate the right number of vehicles and guides.

Can I speak to someone before booking?

Yes. WhatsApp connects you directly with the local VOY PRO team.
