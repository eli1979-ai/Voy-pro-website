BUDAPEST FOR GROUPS

A shared city experience that is easy to organize and hard to forget

EZRaider gives a group the feeling of moving through Budapest together instead of spending the day transferring between vehicles or walking long distances. It works especially well when you want sightseeing to feel social and active while keeping the logistics in one place.

Built around the real group size

We coordinate vehicles, guides and escorts around the group you actually have. When one departure is not the practical solution, the local team can plan multiple coordinated departures subject to live vehicle and staff availability.

One enquiry, one local coordinator

Send the preferred date, approximate headcount and the kind of Budapest experience you want. We’ll help choose the most suitable route and confirm the current operating setup directly with you.

Good for mixed groups

Families and mixed-age groups can tell us how many independent riders, child passengers and baby passengers are joining. Current rider and passenger eligibility is confirmed from the operating policy.

GROUP TOUR FAQ

Questions we usually answer for Budapest groups.

What is the best way to arrange a larger group?

Contact the Budapest team on WhatsApp with your date and approximate headcount. Larger groups often need coordination beyond a normal online booking, so we check the current vehicles, guides and departure structure directly.

Can the experience be private for our group?

Private group options are subject to current availability. Tell us your preferred date and time and we’ll confirm the practical format.

Can you handle a group that does not fit one departure?

Yes, when the live vehicle and staffing setup allows it, the team can coordinate more than one departure so the wider group follows one organized plan.

What are the age rules?

Current Budapest age rules are loaded from our operating policy.

Do riders need previous experience?

No. Every tour starts with a briefing and practice, and the group departs only when riders are comfortable.

What happens if the weather changes?

Tours can operate in light rain. If conditions affect your group plan, the local team will coordinate the relevant timing or change options under the active booking policy.
