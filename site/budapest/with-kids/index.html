FAMILY-FRIENDLY BUDAPEST

Why families choose an EZRaider tour in Budapest

Child passenger seats

For younger children, we use the Thule Yepp 2 Maxi, typically for ages 1–3 in our Budapest setup. For older children, we use the Thule Yepp Junior. Tell us each child’s age when booking so the local team can confirm the right seat, fit and current availability before departure.

Less walking on Castle Hill

Buda Castle and Fisherman’s Bastion are spectacular, but the hills, cobblestones and distances can make a family sightseeing day tiring. The EZRaider lets the family move between the main stops together, with guided pauses for stories, views and photos.

Training before every departure

Every independent rider receives a briefing and practice before the tour starts. Independent-riding and child-passenger eligibility always follow the current booking policy and the setup confirmed for your family.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions before booking.

Do I need previous experience?

No. Every tour starts with a briefing and practice, and the group departs only when everyone is comfortable.

What are the age rules?

Current Budapest age rules are loaded from our operating policy.

What happens if the weather changes?

Tours can operate in light rain. If conditions affect departure, the local team will present the relevant timing or change options. The policy shown for the booking governs.

What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?

The cancellation and rescheduling terms shown during booking apply to your reservation.

Can I book a private tour or a large group?

Yes, subject to availability. For larger groups, use the group page and our local team will coordinate the right number of vehicles and guides.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, subject to the child’s age, the active passenger-seat policy and seat availability. Tell us the children’s ages when booking and the local team will confirm the appropriate setup.

Which EZRaider route is best for families?

For many first-time families, the Buda Castle and Fisherman’s Bastion route is the strongest choice because it combines major sights and viewpoints while reducing the amount of walking across Castle Hill. Margaret Island is a calmer, greener alternative.

Can I speak to someone before booking?

Yes. WhatsApp connects you directly with the local VOY PRO team.
