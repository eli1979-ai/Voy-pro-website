**Why the EZRaider?**

Discover Budapest on a guided EZRaider experience. Our four-wheel electric vehicles offer a different way to enjoy historic streets and scenic viewpoints. Tours follow routes approved by our local team and are subject to road conditions, weather, group needs and applicable restrictions.

**Who can take part?**

Riders are generally aged 16 or over, and participation depends on the safety briefing and the team's assessment. A child-passenger seat may be available in suitable circumstances by advance arrangement. Please contact us before visiting to confirm the appropriate option for your group. Never assume that infants or all ages can participate.

**Accessibility and individual requirements**

If you have mobility limitations or other specific needs, please get in touch before booking. Our team will discuss the practical requirements and assess whether an available tour can accommodate you safely. We cannot guarantee that every rider or route will be suitable.

Discover highlights such as the Buda Castle district, Heroes' Square and Margaret Island, with time for selected stops and viewpoints. Longer and specialist routes are offered only when confirmed by the Budapest team. Sending a WhatsApp message is an enquiry, not a confirmed reservation.

Budapest visitors looking for a memorable guided activity, families seeking an age-appropriate option, groups of friends and couples. Suitability, routes and availability are confirmed individually.
