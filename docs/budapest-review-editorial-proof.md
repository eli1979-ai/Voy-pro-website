### English (en)

A huge recommendation for EZRaider in Budapest!

We went on an EZRaider tour on Margaret Island and the Buda side, and it was simply incredible!

We were a group of five families, five people in each family, spanning an impressive range of ages, from one and a half to 40, and everyone, young and old, enjoyed it just as much!

We arrived during very hot days and were a little worried about coping with the heat, but we quickly discovered that this is actually one of the most fun and comfortable ways to see Budapest. Instead of walking long distances in the heat, you ride an EZRaider, enjoy the breeze and the journey, and manage to see so much of the city in a relatively short time.

The route is beautiful, taking you through landscapes and places we might not have reached or even discovered on a regular tour. For us, it was also the perfect kind of “introduction tour” to the city: during the ride we spotted places we loved, then returned to them on foot at our own pace.

And a huge word of praise for the team — simply wonderful!

Especially Liori and Yehuda, who were lovely, patient, pleasant and fun, and made the experience even better.

An excellent experience for families, even a large group with a wide age range like ours. A unique, easygoing and enjoyable way to get to know Budapest and see much more of the city.

We wholeheartedly recommend it!
